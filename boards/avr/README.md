# `avr` Architecture Boards (`boards/avr`)

## Overview
Intermediate blueprint grouping board support packages (BSPs) and SoC/MCU families for the `avr` architecture (4 chip/family subdirectories).

## Subdirectories & Boundaries
- [`at32uc3`](./at32uc3/README.md) - Intermediate blueprint grouping board support packages and shared board drivers for the `at32uc3` SoC/MCU family under the `avr` architecture (2 subdirectories).
- [`at90usb`](./at90usb/README.md) - Intermediate blueprint grouping board support packages and shared board drivers for the `at90usb` SoC/MCU family under the `avr` architecture (2 subdirectories).
- [`atmega`](./atmega/README.md) - Intermediate blueprint grouping board support packages and shared board drivers for the `atmega` SoC/MCU family under the `avr` architecture (5 subdirectories).
- [`avrdx`](./avrdx/README.md) - Intermediate blueprint grouping board support packages and shared board drivers for the `avrdx` SoC/MCU family under the `avr` architecture (1 subdirectories).
