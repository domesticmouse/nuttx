# Signal Subsystem (`sched/signal`)

## Overview
The `sched/signal` component implements the NuttX POSIX and real-time signal subsystem (`CONFIG_ENABLE_ALL_SIGNALS` and minimal kernel signal support). It manages pre-allocated and dynamic pools of signal action descriptors (`sigactq_t`), pending signal queue entries (`sigq_t`), and group-pending signal records (`sigpendq_t`); registers and invokes default and user-defined signal handlers (`sigaction`, `nxsig_deliver`); dispatches thread-directed and process-group-directed signals (`kill`, `tgkill`, `sigqueue`, `nxsig_dispatch`, `nxsig_tcbdispatch`, `nxsig_notification`); maintains per-thread signal masks (`sigprocmask`, `sigpending`, `sigsuspend`, `pause`); and implements signal-aware waiting and sleeping primitives (`sigtimedwait`, `sigwaitinfo`, `clock_nanosleep`, `nxsig_nanosleep`, `nxsig_sleep`, `nxsig_usleep`, `ppoll`, `pselect`).

## Interface
- `signal.h:g_sigfreeaction`, `signal.h:g_sigpendingaction`, `signal.h:g_sigpendingirqaction`, `signal.h:g_sigpendingsignal`, `signal.h:g_sigpendingirqsignal` - Global free queues for signal action entries, pending signal actions, and pending signals (including dedicated interrupt-handler pools).
- `signal.h:nxsig_initialize` (`sig_initialize.c:nxsig_initialize`) - Initializes the global signal pools (`g_sigactalloc`, `g_sigpendactionalloc`, `g_sigpendingirqactionalloc`, `g_sigpendsignalalloc`, `g_sigpendingirqsignalalloc`) during OS startup.
- `sig_action.c:nxsig_action`, `sig_action.c:sigaction`, `signal.h:nxsig_release_action` (`sig_action.c:nxsig_release_action`), `signal.h:nxsig_find_action` (`sig_findaction.c:nxsig_find_action`) - Queries or installs per-group signal handlers (`struct sigaction`), manages `SA_NOCLDWAIT` on `SIGCHLD`, and looks up or releases `sigactq_t` entries.
- `signal.h:nxsig_isdefault` (`sig_default.c:nxsig_isdefault`), `signal.h:nxsig_iscatchable` (`sig_default.c:nxsig_iscatchable`), `signal.h:nxsig_default` (`sig_default.c:nxsig_default`), `signal.h:nxsig_default_initialize` (`sig_default.c:nxsig_default_initialize`) - Evaluates catchability (`SIGKILL` and `SIGSTOP` cannot be caught or ignored) and configures default signal actions (`SIG_ACT_TERMINATE`, `SIG_ACT_STOP`, `SIG_ACT_CONTINUE`, `SIG_ACT_IGNORE`).
- `signal.h:nxsig_deliver` (`sig_deliver.c:nxsig_deliver`) - Dequeues pending `sigq_t` signal actions on a target thread, masks `sa_mask` (and the signal itself unless `SA_NODEFER`), invokes the kernel or user-space signal handler (`up_sigdeliver`), and restores the original `sigprocmask`.
- `sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_tcbdispatch`, `sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_dispatch`, `sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_source_queued` - Dispatches a `siginfo_t` event to a target TCB or task group, waking blocked waiters (`TSTATE_WAIT_SIG`, `TSTATE_WAIT_SEM`, `TSTATE_WAIT_MQNOTEMPTY`, `TSTATE_WAIT_MQNOTFULL`, `TSTATE_TASK_STOPPED`), scheduling `up_schedule_sigaction` when unmasked, or queuing on `group->tg_sigpendingq` when masked.
- `sig_kill.c:nxsig_kill`, `sig_kill.c:kill`, `sig_tgkill.c:tgkill`, `sig_queue.c:nxsig_queue`, `sig_queue.c:sigqueue`, `sig_notification.c:nxsig_notification`, `sig_notification.c:nxsig_notification_source`, `sig_notification.c:nxsig_cancel_notification` - Sends signals or `sigevent` notifications (including `SIGEV_SIGNAL` and `SIGEV_THREAD` workqueue callbacks) to processes, threads, or process groups.
- `sig_procmask.c:nxsig_procmask`, `sig_procmask.c:sigprocmask`, `sig_pending.c:nxsig_pendingset`, `sig_pending.c:sigpending`, `sig_lowest.c:nxsig_lowest`, `signal.h:nxsig_unmask_pendingsignal` (`sig_unmaskpendingsignal.c:nxsig_unmask_pendingsignal`), `signal.h:nxsig_restoremask` (`sig_unmaskpendingsignal.c:nxsig_restoremask`) - Inspects or updates a thread's blocked signal mask, computes the union of thread-pending and group-pending signals, and dispatches newly unmasked signals.
- `sig_timedwait.c:nxsig_timedwait`, `sig_timedwait.c:sigtimedwait`, `sig_clockwait.c:nxsig_clockwait`, `sig_waitinfo.c:nxsig_waitinfo`, `sig_waitinfo.c:sigwaitinfo`, `sig_suspend.c:sigsuspend`, `sig_pause.c:pause`, `signal.h:nxsig_timeout` (`sig_timedwait.c:nxsig_timeout`) - Blocks the calling thread in `TSTATE_WAIT_SIG` on `g_waitingforsignal` until a waited signal arrives or a watchdog timer (`rtcb->waitdog`) expires.
- `sig_nanosleep.c:nxsig_nanosleep`, `sig_nanosleep.c:clock_nanosleep`, `sig_sleep.c:nxsig_sleep`, `sig_usleep.c:nxsig_usleep`, `sig_ppoll.c:ppoll`, `sig_pselect.c:pselect` - Suspends execution or polls file descriptors with temporary signal masks and nanosecond/tick timeouts.
- `signal.h:nxsig_alloc_pendingsigaction` (`sig_allocpendingsigaction.c:nxsig_alloc_pendingsigaction`), `signal.h:nxsig_release_pendingsigaction` (`sig_releasependingsigaction.c:nxsig_release_pendingsigaction`), `signal.h:nxsig_release_pendingsignal` (`sig_releasependingsignal.c:nxsig_release_pendingsignal`), `signal.h:nxsig_remove_pendingsignal` (`sig_removependingsignal.c:nxsig_remove_pendingsignal`), `signal.h:nxsig_cleanup` (`sig_cleanup.c:nxsig_cleanup`), `signal.h:nxsig_discard_threadsignals` (`sig_cleanup.c:nxsig_discard_threadsignals`), `signal.h:nxsig_release` (`sig_cleanup.c:nxsig_release`) - Manages lifecycle and cleanup of per-TCB and per-group signal action and pending signal queues.

## Invariants
- Non-catchable signals (`SIGKILL` and `SIGSTOP`, checked via `sig_default.c:nxsig_iscatchable`) cannot have custom handlers installed in `sig_action.c:nxsig_action` (returns `-EINVAL`) and are silently stripped from `sigprocmask` modifications in `sig_procmask.c:nxsig_procmask`.
- During an active system call (`(rtcb->flags & TCB_FLAG_SYSCALL) != 0`), user-space signal delivery is deferred (`sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_queue_action`), uninterruptible semaphore waits (`TCB_FLAG_UNINTERRUPTIBLE`) are not aborted by signals (`sig_dispatch.c`), and temporary signal masks from `sigsuspend`, `ppoll`, or `pselect` are preserved via `TCB_FLAG_SIGMASK_SAVED` (`sig_unmaskpendingsignal.c:nxsig_restoremask`) until syscall exit.
- Interrupt-context signal dispatches (`up_interrupt_context()`) never allocate from the kernel heap (`kmm_malloc`), drawing exclusively from `g_sigpendingaction`/`g_sigpendingirqaction` and `g_sigpendingsignal`/`g_sigpendingirqsignal` inside a critical section (`sig_allocpendingsigaction.c:nxsig_alloc_pendingsigaction`, `sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_alloc_pendingsignal`).
- At most one pending instance per standard signal number (`signo < SIGRTMIN`) is queued in `group->tg_sigpendingq` (`sig_dispatch.c:nxsig_add_pendingsignal`), and unmasked pending signals are drained in ascending signal-number order (`sig_lowest.c:nxsig_lowest`, `sig_unmaskpendingsignal.c:nxsig_dispatch_unmasked`).

## Side Effects
- Modifies task states (`TSTATE_WAIT_SIG`, `TSTATE_TASK_STOPPED`, ready-to-run queues) and global queues (`g_waitingforsignal`, `g_stoppedtasks`), arms/cancels watchdog timers (`wd_start_abstick`, `wd_cancel`), and triggers context switches (`up_switch_context`) or architecture signal trampolines (`up_schedule_sigaction`).
- Default kernel signal actions (`sig_default.c:nxsig_stop_task`, `sig_default.c:nxsig_kill_task`, `sig_default.c:nxsig_abnormal_termination`) can suspend or terminate threads/processes via `nxtask_exit`, `pthread_exit`, or `_exit`.
- Allocates and frees signal descriptors from static pools or `kmm_malloc`/`kmm_free` (`SIG_ALLOC_FIXED`, `SIG_ALLOC_IRQ`, `SIG_ALLOC_DYN`) and schedules `LPWORK` work items for `SIGEV_THREAD` notifications (`sig_notification.c:nxsig_notification_source`).
- Sets thread-local `errno` (`set_errno`) in POSIX wrapper entry points (`sigaction`, `kill`, `tgkill`, `sigqueue`, `sigprocmask`, `sigpending`, `sigsuspend`, `pause`, `sigtimedwait`, `sigwaitinfo`, `ppoll`, `pselect`).
